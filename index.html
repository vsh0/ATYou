What is ATYou?


ATYOU is a project created by students, for students, with a special focus on supporting neurodivergent individuals. We know that stepping into college life can feel overwhelming, especially when you’re unsure what to expect. That’s why we built ATYOU: to make the transition into third-level education at ATU easier for everyone!
