Mental Health

Take care of yourself—visit the Health Unit if you ever need support or medical help.


The Student Health Unit provides medical services to students on campus free of charge! Located in the Students Union area, the Unit is staffed by a team of nurses and doctors who have extensive experience in student health issues. They offer an acute illness and energency service for students who fall ill while in college. All information provided to the doctor or nurse is kept confidential and not discolsed to any other person without permission from the student. Records are kept on a separate network to the main system and ae not shared.



To avail of the Student Health services, you must first complete this form. The Unit operates on a an appointment basis. Contact the Health Unit using the below number or email address to schedule an appointment. On receipt of your request for an appointment, a nurse will ring you to discuss the reason for your call.
