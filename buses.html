Most bus routes collect and drop off at the front of the college entrance, as shown below in the diagram – the 402, 404, and 409 all drop and collect from the spot circled purple below. Do note also, that the 401 drops and collects from the pink circled area below, this route is a handy one to know if you’re located towards the and it stretches all the way to Salthill – all the way to ATU’s doorstep!
