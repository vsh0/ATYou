Academic Supports

Don’t hesitate to reach out for help—academic support is here to guide you through tough assignments and exams.


ATU offers a range of academic supports to students who have disablities or who are struggling with certain aspects of college life. There is always someone you can turn to for help

Disability Support



The Disability and Learning Support Service provides a range of supports and services for students within the university. They provide support to students with physical disabilities, sensory impairment, mental health difficulties, specific learning difficulties, neurological and ongoing illnesses and ADHD and Autistic students. You can register with the service by filling out this form. If you have applied through DARE, you do not need to do anything, a member of learning support staff will get in touch with you to arrange a needs assessment. Supports may include learning support, assistive technology or accommodations for teaching, learning and assessment. Specific supports will be discussed during the needs assessment appointment.
