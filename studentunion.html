Student Union

Get to know your Student Union — they're here to support you, represent you, and help you make the most of your college experience.


The ATU Students’ Union (ATUSU) is a democratic organisation run by students, for students. They organise lots of fun events throughout the year, and are also a valuable source of support, so don't be afraid to go to them if you need help with anything. The Students' Union can assist you with issues in areas such as grants, timetables, exams, lectures, personal issues and more. Click here to go to the official ATUSU website. Here you can find a wealth of information about the Union members' roles and the services they offer.
