Walking Information

If you live close enough to campus to walk – lucky you! And it’s great for your mental health to take a walk for yourself before and after the college day, to maybe listen to some music or a podcast and mentally prepare or mentally cool down rom your day. The campus has many entry points as marked below, so it’s best to find the one most suitable depending on which direction you are walking from, or where your first class of the day takes place.
